How it works

Sending gives the application a number and notifies the first person in the approval chain. The steps run in order: the second person cannot approve until the first has approved, and the third cannot approve until the second has approved. Each person ticks “Use my signature” — the signature they saved in My settings — before they can approve or reject, and their signature appears on the application and its PDF, under their step's role. The APPROVED seal only appears once the last person in the chain has approved it — an approval by an earlier person shows no seal. If a person rejects, their block says “Rejected By” above their name and the REJECTED stamp appears on the PDF at once, and the request goes no further. A step set to None is skipped, and no signature block appears for it. You are notified at each step and can see it in "My applications". The PDF is a formal letter, not an invoice.
