A new application starts as Pending, with the first person in its approval chain. Once they approve it, it is Checked and goes to the next person; the last person's approval or rejection decides it. The APPROVED seal or REJECTED stamp only appears on the PDF once the last person has decided. The application number (LV-0001) is the official number to quote. The share code (HRC-#####) is only for scanning and searching — it is not the application number.

How approval works

Each person in the approval chain adds their signature — the signature they saved in My settings — which appears on the application and its PDF, under their step's role. Nobody can save a decision until their signature is on file, and nobody can act until the person before them has approved. Other users can open and see an application, but not change it.
