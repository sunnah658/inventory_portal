A new application starts as Pending, with the department user you sent it to. Once they check it and forward it to HR & Admin, it is Checked. Only the HR approver and the HR & Admin department admin can approve or reject it — the applicant and the checker are notified of every change. The application number (LV-0001) is the official number to quote. The share code (HRC-#####) is only for scanning and searching — it is not the application number.

How approval works

When the checker forwards an application, they add their signature — the signature they saved in My settings — which appears on the application and its PDF, under Checked By. When HR & Admin approve it, they add their signature the same way, which appears under Approved By. The HR approver cannot save the status until their signature is on file. Only the HR approver and the HR & Admin department admin can change its status. Other HR & Admin users can open and see it, but not change it.
