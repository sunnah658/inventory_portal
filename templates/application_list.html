Application Letters

Apply for leave using one of the letter kinds below, or write a custom application with your own subject and body. You pick up to three approvers — each a Checked By, Approved By or Authorised By step, from your department, HR & Admin or Management — and the steps run in order. Each person must tick “Use my signature” before they can approve or reject.

A new application starts as Pending, with the first person in its approval chain. Once they approve it, it is Checked and goes to the next person; the last person's approval or rejection decides it. The APPROVED seal or REJECTED stamp only appears on the PDF once the last person has decided. The application number (LV-0001) is the official number to quote. The share code (HRC-#####) is only for scanning and searching — it is not the application number. Only the applicant, the people in the approval chain, their department admin and the portal admin can open an application.
