How reminders work

A reminder date on a record warns its owner 1 day before and on the day itself (from 7:00 in the morning), plus once more at the number of days you choose in Notifications.

Department Database meetings warn the row owner and every department admin 7, 3 and 1 days before, and again on the morning of the meeting.
