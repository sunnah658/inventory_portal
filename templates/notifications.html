Who gets told what

Nobody is told about the portal admin's tasks. The portal admin is told about permission-user and department-admin tasks. A permission user is told about department-admin tasks. A department admin is told when a user in their department adds, edits, shares or deletes a Department Database row, creates an invoice draft, finalises an invoice, changes an invoice's status to Approved or Pending, deletes an invoice, sends an Item Request, edits a request, or when a request from their department has its status changed.

Applications: the department user an application is sent to is told when it arrives; the applicant is told when the checker forwards it to HR & Admin and when HR approves or rejects it; the checker is told when HR approves or rejects it.

Editing an invoice draft, or changing a public QR, tells nobody.
