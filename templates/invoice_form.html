Who approves it — 3 steps, in order

Pick who approves this invoice, from any department. Finalise starts it forwarding through the steps in order: the second person cannot approve until the first has approved, and the third cannot start until the second has approved. Each person must tick “Use my signature” before they can approve or reject. If a person rejects, the invoice stops there and the REJECTED stamp appears on the PDF; the APPROVED seal appears only when the last person approves.

How it works

Live totals update as you type. Save draft to keep working on it. Finalise locks the invoice, gives it a number and starts it forwarding through the steps you picked — the status becomes Pending. The APPROVED seal appears only when the last person approves; if anyone rejects, the invoice stops there and the REJECTED stamp appears. An issued invoice keeps the format it was made with.
