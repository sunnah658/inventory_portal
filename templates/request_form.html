Who approves it — 3 steps, in order

Pick who approves this request, from your department, HR & Admin or Management. Exactly one step must be Send/Request To — the person who receives the request. Sending starts it forwarding through the steps in order: the second person cannot approve until the first has approved, and the third cannot start until the second has approved. Each person must tick “Use my signature” before they can approve or reject. If a person rejects, the request stops there and the REJECTED stamp appears on the PDF; the APPROVED seal appears only when the last person approves, and then the Send/Request To user can change the status to Pending, Issued, Paid or Handovered.

How it works

Live totals update as you type. Sending gives the request a number and starts it forwarding through the steps you picked. The first person is notified at once; the Send/Request To user is told at their turn, sees it in "Other's request", can print it and download its PDF, and — once the last person has approved — changes the status. You are notified of the change and can see it in "My request".
